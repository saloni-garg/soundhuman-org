FAQ

Questions, answered

How is this different from preference data or a taste dataset?

Preference data teaches a model what people like. SpecGrade teaches it what is correct. Every reward comes from deterministic checks against a written specification: exact text, geometry, colour, preservation, legal rules. A model judge is capped at a fifth of the reward and human preference is only a tie-break.

What exactly do we download?

A frozen, versioned release: graded tasks with defect-verified starting images, professional repair trajectories recorded as model-level edit sequences, the verifier itself, train and eval splits, published lazy-baseline scores, documentation, and a changelog. Everything needed to train and to reproduce the run later.

Can our model just learn to game the verifier?

That is the first thing we test for. Every release is attacked with adversarial rollouts and lazy baselines before it ships, gates cap the reward when a hard rule fails, and the private held-out eval rotates continuously, so overfitting the public set shows up immediately in the numbers that matter.

How do the environments stay current?

Platform and marketplace rules are tracked continuously and folded into a new frozen release every quarter, with migration notes explaining every change. Failure mining is refreshed after every major model release, so new failure modes become new tasks.

We do not train models. Is there anything here for us?

Yes: Continuous Evaluation. You get clause-by-clause compliance reports on any image model you run or buy, a live leaderboard, drift alerts, and a quarterly benchmark, with no training data involved and nothing to install.

Can we evaluate SpecGrade before licensing?

Every plan starts with the full sample pack: complete tasks, trajectories, and verifier outputs from all six environments, free. Most teams score their current model against it within days and know exactly which environment they need.
