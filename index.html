      04
      How it works
      Four jobs
      The full skill -- inside Claude, Codex, Cursor and more

      One profile.
      Four jobs.

      A

      Calibrate

      Give it three things you wrote. It measures how you write and notes your openings, closings, and marks. You correct the profile once.

      B

      Write

      It writes new drafts from your VOICE.md. It uses short form with your team, full sentences outside, and formal words when the reader needs them.

      C

      Rewrite

      It keeps your meaning and your facts. It changes only the words, so the draft sounds like you wrote it.

      D

      Check

      It gives a verdict and shows the exact lines that drift, with fixes. No score on its own. If there is no profile, it will not make one up.

      The profile is the thing you keep. Tools change. A VOICE.md file in plain text works in every agent. It gets better each time you correct it.

      A HUMANIZER

      Removes AI tells, once

      • Removes common AI tells from one draft
      • Same result for every writer
      • Would "fix" your shorthand, your marks, your quirks -- the things that prove it is you
      • Nothing is saved. The next draft starts from zero
      WRITE LIKE ME

      Learns your voice and keeps it

      • Builds a voice profile from your writing and keeps it
      • Different for every writer -- because it is yours
      • One profile per way you write: team, outside, formal, public
      • Lives in a VOICE.md file you own and correct

      Sounding less like AI is not enough. The draft has to sound like you. That is why the profile matters more than the cleanup.
